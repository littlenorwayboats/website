# Our new spot

This is our new home. We picked up a slip at the Port of Poulsbo, on D Dock, right in front of the Brass Kraken.

![Marina map of the Port of Poulsbo, with our slip on D Dock circled beside the Brass Kraken.](/images/blog/port-of-poulsbo.png)

We're starting in 2027, with a soft launch this December.

The boat is in dry storage for now. In December it makes its way down to the dock.
