# Your First Electric Boat Rental on Liberty Bay: What to Know

Renting a boat in Poulsbo doesn't take a captain's license or a lifetime on the water. Our electric Duffy is one of the simplest boats you'll ever drive. Here's what to expect, from booking to casting off.

## Do I need boating experience?

No. The boat tops out around 6 mph and steers much like a golf cart. Your driver needs to be 25 or older with a valid driver's license in hand. Before you leave the dock, we walk them through a short safety briefing and a hands-on tutorial.

## How many people can come?

Up to 10 guests, including the driver and any children. All ages are welcome. Infants and kids count toward the limit, so plan your crew list accordingly.


## What should we bring?

Picnics, snacks, and drinks are welcome, and the boat has a Bluetooth stereo for your playlist. Leave glass cups and breakable drinkware at home. Wine bottles are fine if you handle them with care. Adult life jackets are stowed under the seats and we keep child and toddler sizes on hand, but if your kids have a favorite vest, bring it.

## Where do we park and meet?

Use the public parking near downtown Poulsbo and the Port of Poulsbo waterfront. Spaces fill up fast on sunny weekends and festival days, so give yourself extra time. There's no separate office. You check in at our slip on D Dock, right in front of the Brass Kraken, and exact directions arrive in your confirmation email.

Plan to arrive about 10 minutes before your start time. That leaves room for paperwork and the skipper's orientation without cutting into your cruise.

## Where can we go?

Liberty Bay, within the harbor limits we outline at check-in. It's a scenic coastal cruise: no shoreline docking, no shallow areas, and no trips out into the open sound. On a clear day, keep an eye out for Mount Rainier, and for the seals that live in the bay.

## What about the weather?

If wind or visibility makes the harbor unsafe, we'll contact you, and you can reschedule or get a full refund. For any other change of plans, cancel at least 48 hours ahead for a full refund.
