# Christmas in Poulsbo 2026: Lights, Nordic Cheer & Getting Here from Seattle

When the days grow short and the Pacific Northwest settles into the darkest stretch of the year, Poulsbo comes alive with unmistakable Norwegian charm. Tucked along the shores of Liberty Bay, Washington’s “Little Norway” wraps itself in thousands of glowing Christmas lights—and this holiday season, getting here is easier than ever. With the [FRS Clipper’s seasonal Poulsbo Express](https://poulsbo-express.frs-clipper.com/) bringing day-trippers straight across the water from downtown Seattle to Poulsbo’s waterfront, there has never been a better time to soak up the small-town Nordic cheer.

Downtown Poulsbo is dressed to the nines in lush holiday garlands, twinkling storefronts, and festive cheer, complete with Nordic Santa holding court in his cozy little hut on Front Street. Down at the marina, boats along the water are decked out in sparkling holiday lights, casting shimmering reflections across Liberty Bay—a sight best enjoyed on an evening drive down Fjord Drive to take in all the waterfront decorations.

![Rainbow over Scandinavian-style buildings on Front Street in downtown Poulsbo, with Marina Market and the street clock after a rain shower.](/images/blog/downtown-poulsbo-rainbow-front-street.jpg)

Whether you are arriving by Clipper or cruising along the bay, mark your calendar for these can’t-miss **2026 Poulsbo holiday events**:

## Poulsbo Tree Lighting

Kick off the season on **Friday, November 27, 2026**, from 5:30 PM to 7:00 PM at [Muriel Iverson Williams Waterfront Park](https://poulsbo.gov/parks-recreation/) in Historic Downtown Poulsbo. This free, family-friendly gathering is the official start of the holiday season on Liberty Bay.

## Nordic Santa on Front Street

Visit Santa at Santa’s House on Front Street on Saturdays and Sundays from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, starting Thanksgiving weekend (**November 28, 2026**) through the weekend before Christmas.

## Poulsbo Heritage Museum Holiday Market

Browse local gifts and seasonal crafts at the [Poulsbo Historical Society](https://poulsbohistory.org/) Holiday Market on **November 27–29** and again **December 18–20**, typically 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

## Poulsbo Bloody Mary Krawl

Sip and stroll through downtown stops on **Sunday, November 29, 2026**, from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Check [VISIT Poulsbo](https://visitpoulsbo.com/) for participating stops and tickets.

## Annual Julefest Celebration & Solstice Bonfire

Experience the heart of Poulsbo’s Nordic heritage on **Saturday, December 5, 2026**, at [Poulsbo Sons of Norway](https://www.poulsbosonsofnorway.com/julefest.html) (18991 Front St NE). Enjoy the Nordic market, traditional treats, and a dramatic dusk celebration featuring the arrival of the Lucia Bride and a roaring waterfront bonfire with Vikings—one of the year’s not-to-miss nights on Liberty Bay. Clipper travelers: look for the special 12:00 PM Seattle departure that day so you can stay for the evening festivities.

## Breakfast with Santa at Sons of Norway

Enjoy a festive Norwegian pancake breakfast with Santa on **Sunday, December 6, 2026**, from 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM at [Sons of Norway](https://www.poulsbosonsofnorway.com/). The public is welcome.

## 57th Annual Poulsbo Yacht Club Lighted Ships Parade

Gather along Muriel Iverson Williams Waterfront Park or the marina boardwalk on **Saturday, December 12, 2026**, from 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM. Watch glowing, decorated boats cruise Liberty Bay in one of Poulsbo’s most magical—and completely free—holiday traditions, put on by the [Poulsbo Yacht Club](https://poulsboyachtclub.org/page/lighted-ships).
